Selected AI Work

  • Mongolian drama dubbing model (trained myself). Built a ~92-hour, 95-speaker Mongolian speech corpus and fine-tuned a voice model on it, including emotion training (angry, sad, tender delivery). Built per-character voice casting that keeps one consistent voice per character across a series, and an automated QA gate that checks every line for correct words, voice, gender, pitch, timing and a model-based listening test that matches human review 87-93% of the time. Built the subtitle side too: it reads the source subtitles (official tracks, or burned-in text read from the video frames), rewrites them as natural Mongolian sized to each line's timing, and burns Mongolian subtitles into the final episode. Watch: nasanochir.github.io/mongolian-ai-dubbing
  • Grounded civic AI (Open Parliament, 3rd place). Search and chat over parliament agendas, transcripts and 950 laws (15k articles), built only on official open government data. Missing data is shown as a gap, never filled in by the model. Facts are kept only when backed by a verbatim official quote. A validation rule threw out 345 of 550 wrong document links. The assistant re-answers under a stricter grounding rule when it fails to cite sources.
  • Paid LLM product (astro.mn). 14 personalized long-form reading types and a chat that remembers the user. Facts are computed in code and locked into the prompt so the model can't invent numbers. Live with paid checkout.
  • Mongolian language tools. Mongolian meeting transcription and AI meeting notes SaaS (ZeroTech). A spell checker with 96% top-1 suggestion accuracy against 72% for the market leader, on a held-out benchmark.
  • AI adoption. Designed Shunkhlai Group's 4-module employee AI training program. Use multi-agent coding workflows day to day.
